Many people ask for authentic Vietnamese food in Korea, so I decided to write down the places I've tried. They come pretty close to the dishes in my homeland, and most are owned or run by Vietnamese people.

1. **Lẩu (hotpot):** 하노이맛집 (총정로). Review (Korean): <https://m.blog.naver.com/bravo424/221755634578>
2. **Bánh mì, bún đậu mắm tôm, lòng dồi...:** Phở Hằng (PhoHang), 서울 종로구 창신길 15. Map: <http://naver.me/xeH89iZI>
3. **Cơm sườn, bánh mì:** Phở Hiền (near Ewha Womans University)
4. **Bánh mì:** 63 PROV (near Ewha Womans University)
