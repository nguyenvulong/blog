"A Thousand Years" is a song by Christina Perri, written for the soundtrack of *The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 1*. It is a gentle love song about waiting for someone, finding the courage to love, and being "one step closer" to them.

The lyrics are copyrighted, so I have not reproduced them here. You can listen to the song and read the lyrics on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=christina+perri+a+thousand+years) or on an official lyrics site.
