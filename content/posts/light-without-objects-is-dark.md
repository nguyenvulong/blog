["Light without objects, is dark. Objects without light, are dark."](https://www.quora.com/Why-does-the-Suns-ray-brighten-up-the-Earth-and-outer-space-still-remain-dark/answers/312889559?no_redirect=1)

Đây là một câu trả lời ngắn nhưng hay của Zane Scheepers trên Quora:

> To perceive brightness, 2 things are required. Light, and, something to reflect the light to our eyes. Outer space only has one of those. Light isn't bright. Objects which emit or reflect light, can appear bright.
